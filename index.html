THE WORLD RACE

NINE MONTHS.
ONE MISSION.

The World Race is a nine-month mission journey where young adults travel to different countries, live in community with a team, and spend their time serving others and sharing the love of Jesus.

I'll be traveling through South Africa, Eswatini, Kenya, Ethiopia, Nepal, and Thailand, serving alongside others and learning what it looks like to live on mission.
